# SAS, SASU, SA and SARL governance: ready-to-use templates

Organigram provides templates for the main French commercial-company forms:
SAS, SASU, SA and SARL/EURL. They provide an initial governance structure and
the corporate records that can be configured for the company’s actual needs.

## Create the organigram

<Grid container my={3} justifyContent='center'>
  <Grid item>
    <Link href={'/workspace'} target='_blank'>
      <Button variant='contained'>{t('Launch app')}</Button>
    </Link>
  </Grid>
</Grid>

Click **Add New**, then select the appropriate company template. Select **SAS**
when the company is a société par actions simplifiée; use SASU, SA or SARL/EURL
when that legal form applies.

## Example: SAS

<Minimap organigram={templates.sas} />

The SAS template includes a chairman, executive management, the share-transfer
register, governance procedures and the company records organised into
governance, accounting and compliance layers. Adapt the draft to the bylaws and
the company’s factual situation before deployment.

For day-to-day records, see [Managing corporate registries](/docs/guides/corporate-registries).
