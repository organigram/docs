# Create your company's org chart with AI

Announcing [{process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL}](/), a new tool to draw org charts with AI.

## How it works

1. Sign in with the button on the top right corner of the screen.
2. Head to [the workspace](/workspace) page.
3. Click on "Add New" to open a popup window
4. Choose "Generate from text or docs"
5. Upload the legal documents (e.g. articles of association, shareholder agreements, etc.) and optionally add text instructions to guide the AI or provide additional material.
6. {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} will extract the relevant information and draw the org chart for you
7. You can now edit the org chart, deploy it on a public network, export it as a PDF...

## Why we built it

We built {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} to help founders, investors and legal departments draw org charts faster and more accurately. Linked with the [legal superpowers](/docs/certified/binding-actions) of {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL}, these org charts can represent the real state of your company, in real-time.

We believe compliance should not be a burden, and that legal and financial information should be freely accessible to all authorized to see it. We believe both the authorities and the millions of organizations they oversee could benefit from the gains in transparency and efficiency brought by AI and cryptography.

This is why a simple tool could save millions - if not billions - of hours and resources every year. Of course, org charts are just the first step towards a more comprehensive digital twin of your company. But AI is now here to make that first step as easy as clicking a button.

## How it's built

{process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} is built on top of the [Organigram Protocol](/docs/protocol), which is itself built on top of [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org) and open-weight Large Language Models. The protocol is open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/organigram).
