# Create a multi-organization consortium 🪢

This guide will walk you through the process of creating a multi-organization consortium using the [Organigram Protocol](/docs/protocol).

## Prerequisites

This guide assumes you have already created an organization (whether [📝 a for-profit company](/docs/guides/for-profit) or [🕊️ non-profit organization](/docs/guides/non-profit))

## Create a consortium

To turn your organization into a multi-entity group, you can simply create multiple organigrams in your organization's workspace, each organigram representing a sub-organization. Then, add the relevant organs from each existing sub-organization.

To add an organ which already exists in another organization, simply click on the "Add New Organ" button in the organigram editor, and choose the "Add an existing organ" option.

Then, simply create new procedures showing each type of interactions between these organs and your consortium.

### Example

Let's say you have a non-profit organization called "The Open Source Consortium", and you want to create a consortium with other non-profit organizations.

Your Charter states that the consortium has a seat in the board of directors of each member organization, and inversely, each organization can send one representative to the board of the consortium.

To do so, add the board of directors of all the member organizations to your organigram, and create a procedure (let's call it "Nomination of the consortium representative"). This procedure should be added by the member organization to its board of directors organ, with permissions to add or remove entries.

Inversely, each organization can create a new procedure to add or remove one member from the consortium's board of directors, by adding this organ in their respective organigrams.
