# Connecting to the app 🔐

In order to create and manage incorruptible organizations, we need a way to identify you and allow you to sign transactions; but we want to do it in a way that always keeps you safe and in control of your own data.

## Preserving data and privacy

You can sign in in a self-sovereign, privacy-preserving manner by clicking "Connect Wallet" on the top right corner of the screen. There, you can either:
- Sign in with Passkeys. Select "Porto" in the list. This option will allow you to store private keys in a password manager for use across multiple devices, use biometric credentials, and add [recovery options](https://id.porto.sh). It is the easiest and most secure way to connect to the app.
- Use a crypto wallet such as Rabby, Metamask, Rainbow or Ledger, or any of the 600+ supported wallets via [WalletConnect](https://walletguide.walletconnect.network/).

## Compliance and non-custody

With both options, your private keys and assets are never shared with the app. You can create multiple accounts and switch between them at any time.

That said, you also have the option to verify your identity to get access to [additional features](/docs/advanced/certified) such as [legally-binding e-signatures](/docs/advanced/binding-actions). In this case, we recommend using new addresses that have never been used on a public network before.

For the full list of the information we access and how we use it in these services, you can refer to our [privacy policy](/docs/reference/privacy).

## You are now set!

Head to [the workspace](/workspace) page to create and deploy your first organigram. Feel free to chose [any template](/docs/guides) or add [payment methods](/docs/advanced/transactions) from your Account settings to get started quicker.
