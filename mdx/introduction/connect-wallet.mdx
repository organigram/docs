# Connecting to the app 🔐

Your account is anchored to an Ethereum-compatible address. That address is used
to open your workspace, sign in to the web app, sign governance actions, publish
workspace records, and, when needed, authorize encrypted storage access.

## Choosing a connection method

You can sign in in a self-sovereign, privacy-preserving manner by clicking "Connect Wallet" on the top right corner of the screen. There, you can either:

- Sign in with passkeys. Select "Porto" in the recommended wallet list. This
  option lets you store keys in your password manager, use biometric
  credentials, connect across devices, and configure [recovery
  options](https://id.porto.sh). It is the easiest and recommended option for most new users.
- Use a crypto wallet such as Rabby, MetaMask, Rainbow, Trust Wallet, Ledger, or
  any supported wallet available through
  [WalletConnect](https://walletguide.walletconnect.network/).

## Preserving data and privacy

Both options connect the same kind of account: an address controlled by your own
keys. After you choose a wallet, Organigram asks it to sign a message. During sign-in, Organigram also prepares a personal encryption key for
the connected address when the browser supports it.

The encryption key is separate from your wallet signing key. Your wallet key signs login
messages, transactions, proposals, and other governance actions. The encryption key is only
used to decrypt private workspace content, such as encrypted IPFS files and
encrypted workspace envelopes.

On the server, Organigram verifies the signature before saving the public
encryption key, a key version, and signature on your account. The private encryption key is never sent
to Organigram.

This encryption key is what allows private storage to work without sharing secrets with the
server. Workspace access keys can be wrapped for your public encryption key, and only the
matching private encryption key on your device can unwrap them.

You can manage the encryption key from Account Settings:

- export an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase
- import that backup on another device
- restore access to encrypted workspace content after changing browsers or
  devices

If you do not have the private encryption key on the current device, you may still be
signed in, but private workspace files encrypted for that key cannot be opened
until the backup is restored.


## Compliance and non-custody

With both connection options, your private keys and assets are never shared with
the app. Organigram can ask your wallet to sign messages or transactions, but
the final approval happens in your wallet interface.

You can also verify your identity with an [ID
certificate](/docs/certified/id-certificates) to get access to [advanced
signatures](/docs/certified/advanced-signatures) and [legally-binding
actions](/docs/certified/binding-actions). In that case, we recommend using new
addresses that have never been used on a public network before.

For the full list of the information we access and how we use it in these services, you can refer to our [privacy policy](/docs/reference/privacy).
