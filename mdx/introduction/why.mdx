# Why Organigram 🤔

Corruption is a plague that costs the world around 2% of its GDP. That is a staggering ~2Bn$ each year, with long-term effect that can deeply impact the lives of billions of people.

Technological advancement in the last decades in artificial intelligence and cryptographic networks have already had profound impacts on our lives. They can now easily be used to prove mathematically - in an open, transparent way - that an organization follows a given set of rules.

The technology to take that leap from traditional to automated, mathematically proven organizations is here, but yet the tools to do so are not accessible to the vast majority of people. They are either too complex to use, or too expensive to implement.

This is why Organigram was built, a set of tools to allow anyone to create, manage and operate incorruptible organizations, in a simple, safe and intuitive way.

## Incorruptible organizations

We make org charts slightly differently than everybody else. We structure them so that they can be deployed on a cryptographic network. We use this ability of networks such as Ethereum to prove their integrity in a safe and fully decentralized way.

Organizations deployed on them become "incorruptible" because they are verified simultaneously by thousands of operators, publicly, in the same way that Bitcoin or Ethereum transactions are validated.

We can also use machine learning algorithms to create representations of an organization's structure, before or after they are deployed on a cryptographic network.

Organigrams allow any part or subpart of an organization to be operated on that network, and evolve organically following predetermined rules. They help humans manage complexity and tackle corruption in the age of AI and cryptography.
