# Organigram Certified Program 🛡️

## Certified Entity

Organigram Certified Entity program enables users to create in a single move an organization that has legal force, and to add members that will benefit from Certified services on behalf of the organization. The organization can then perform legally binding actions, sponsor transactions, and store data on decentralized storage.

## Certified Solo

Organigram Certified Solo plans serve the same purpose than Certified Entities, but allow indivudal users to use these services without having to be a member of a particular organization. They can use advanced legal signatures or sponsor their own transactions in the context of a single address. This plan is ideal for independent founders, freelances or individuals wishing to manage sensitive processes while still maintaining complete ownership and control over their data and funds.

### Digital signatures

E-signatures are legally binding digital certificates recognized in most countries around the world, and have been standardized by the eIDAS regulation in Europe. They come in 3 levels of security:

- **Simple e-signature**: the default level of security on {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL}. It is recognized by most countries in the world to perform the most basic legal actions and contract signatures.
- **Advanced e-signature**: a higher level of security that requires a 2-factor authentication to sign a document. It is needed to perform certain actions in some countries, such as the transfer of a company's shares or votes in a shareholders meeting.
- **Qualified e-signature**: the highest level of security that requires a physical device to sign a document. It is required for the most sensitive actions, such as governmental call for tenders or some real estate contracts. They are legally equivalent to handwritten signatures.

Organigram provides **simple e-signatures** by default to all users and optional **advanced e-signatures** for all users subscribed to a Certified Entity or Certified Solo plan.

Advanced signatures provided by Organigram are issued via a certificate authority of the highest qualification level.

### Sponsored transactions

The vast majority of the operations performed on the Organigram platform use a wallet to identify users. Transactions, as opposed to simple signatures, natively require the payment of cryptocurrency to the public network to be performed. Acquiring cryptocurrencies can be cumbersome, while Certified programs enable their users to pay these fees using a simple credit card or bank transfer. You can read more about this in our article about [⚡️ Transactions](/docs/advanced/transactions).

### Binding actions

Most actions taken on our platform are recognized by law. However, as stated in this dedicated article on [⚖️ binding actions](/docs/advanced/binding-actions), European law requires to provide an advanced level of security for some decisions that are particularly sensitive.

This is the case for board meeting decisions, such as the appointment of a CEO, the modification of the company's bylaws, or the approval of financial statements.

Benefiting from such an advanced level of security requires to perform additional checks, such as verifying the identity of individual users, as well as generating a digital signature certificate. These two operations can be performed upon subscription to any Certified plan.

### Decentralized storage

By default, all information and metadata relative to organs, procedures, proposals, and entire organigrams are stored on a decentralized storage service, [IPFS](https://ipfs.io). This enables users to retrieve their data without relying on any other intermediary, and ensure the accessibility and resilience of their data, even if {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} was to stop operating.
