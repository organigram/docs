# Organigram Transactions ⚡️

Many actions in {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} involve sending transactions to a blockchain network. These transactions have a cost associated with them, paid to the network operator to process the transaction.

Natively, they are paid in the native cryptocurrency of the network. This might be a problem because many people don't have or want to own cryptocurrencies, as many (if not all) would benefit from using crytpographic signatures to interact with incorruptible organizations everyday.

A signature is free; casting a vote is too. Deploying organigrams or creating proposals still require some gas.

Thankfully, for even super-small amounts, we can use credit cards or wire transfers to pay for transaction fees directly using Organigram Transactions ⚡️.

## What are transactions?

{process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} uses transactions to record the votes and decisions of its users. This allows {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} to provide a proof of the vote or signature to the user, and to the organization, without having to store any data on our servers.

## What are network fees?

Network fees are the cost of executing a transaction on the blockchain.
The network fee is paid to the network operator to process the transaction on the network.
It depends on the network and the type of transaction, as arbitrary data can be added to the transaction.
The execution of such code is more expensive than a simple transfer of value. It can thus impact the price of the transaction, as well as the network congestion or the price of cryptocurrencies.

## Using Organigram without cryptocurrencies

Using Organigram without cryptocurrencies is as simple as adding payment information once, and never worry about it again 🪄.

The transactions are relayed in a safe and privacy-preserving way to network operators, on your behalf. Your payment information is processed by Stripe. {process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_COMPANY_URL} does not access the transaction data, nor your payment information. We do keep track of the total amount of fees spent, though.
This amount is charged at the end of each month on the saved payment method.

## Sponsored Transactions

Sponsored transactions are transactions that are paid for by a third party. The end user does not have to pay for the transaction fee.
Organigram Transactions allow for an entire Organization to pay for the transaction fees of its users. The organization is charged in Euro and billed once at the end of each billing period. The same rates apply as for any other Organigram Transaction.

You can select a specific organs to use Organigram Transactions. All users that are present in this organ will be able to use Organigram Transactions.

## Getting Started

You need to subscribe to a Certified Plan to use Organigram Transactions. You can do so by clicking [here](/docs/advanced/certified).

## How does it work?

Under the hood, Organigram Transactions are powered by [ERC 4437](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337). The Relayer is a service that relays transactions to the blockchain on your behalf. It is a non-custodial service that is operated in an open market of multiple possible relayers. They do not have access to your private keys, nor do we. Neither of us can access your funds.
