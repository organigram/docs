# Organigram Certified Program 🛡️

Organigram Certified is the paid service layer for organizations and independent operators that need stronger evidence around sensitive decisions.

Use it when a vote, approval, appointment, transaction authorization, or governance change should be tied to identity-backed certificates, advanced signatures, sponsored transactions, certified storage, and durable proof records.

## Certified Solo and Certified Entity

Certified Solo is designed for independent founders, freelancers, and individuals who need Certified services without joining a specific organization.

Certified Entity is designed for companies, associations, cooperatives, and digital organizations that want verified members to use Certified services on behalf of the organization.

Both use the same core Certified services.

## What the program includes

Both plans enable users to execute [Binding actions](/docs/certified/binding-actions): legally-binding automations and records with on-chain evidence. They achieve this by combining the following services:
- [ID certificates](/docs/certified/id-certificates): keep a verified identity linked to the wallet used for governance decisions.
- [Advanced signatures](/docs/certified/advanced-signatures): add stronger signature evidence to sensitive approvals and records.
- [Sponsored transactions](/docs/certified/sponsored-transactions): let users participate without managing cryptocurrency for network fees.
- [Certified storage](/docs/certified/storage): preserve governance metadata and certified proofs on decentralized, encrypted storage infrastructure.

See the [Pricing](/pricing) page for more details on these services.
