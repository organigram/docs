# 🛡️ Organigram Certified Program

Organigram Certified is the set of paid services that give legal weight to the decisions of an organization: verified identities, advanced e-signatures, sponsored transactions, certified storage and durable proof records.

Use it when a vote, approval, appointment, transaction authorization, or governance change should be tied to identity-backed certificates, advanced signatures and proof that can be checked later.

## Who it is for

Certified services run in organizations: companies, associations, cooperatives and digital organizations whose verified members act on their behalf.

Members verify their identity from a Certified organization. Their verification stays attached to them if they leave it, and is reused in any organization they join later. Partners, directors and members invited by an organization pay nothing: it covers their verified identity and their signatures.

Personal workspaces do not run Certified services: they only pay for certified storage and sponsored transactions, as they use them.

## Plans

Every Certified organization subscribes to a plan, monthly without commitment or yearly for less, which includes quotas of verified members, signatures and storage. Usage beyond these quotas is billed at the public prices. Each plan has a maximum number of verified members: beyond it, the organization moves to a larger plan or to a quote.

See the [Pricing](/pricing) page for the plans, their quotas and the public prices.

## What the program includes

Certified services enable users to execute [Binding actions](/docs/certified/binding-actions): legally-binding automations and records with on-chain evidence. They achieve this by combining the following services:
- [ID certificates](/docs/certified/id-certificates): keep a verified identity linked to the wallet used for governance decisions.
- [Advanced signatures](/docs/certified/advanced-signatures): add stronger signature evidence to sensitive approvals and records.
- [Sponsored transactions](/docs/certified/sponsored-transactions): let users participate without managing cryptocurrency for network fees.
- [Certified storage](/docs/certified/storage): preserve governance metadata and certified proofs on decentralized, encrypted storage infrastructure.
