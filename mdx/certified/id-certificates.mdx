# ID Certificates 🪪

ID certificates connect a verified person to the wallet they use on Organigram. They are the foundation for advanced signatures, certified approvals, and governance records that need stronger evidentiary value.

Use them when a decision should not only prove that a wallet signed, but also that the wallet was linked to an identified person at the time of the signature.

## What an ID certificate does

An ID certificate keeps an active certification tied to a user's wallet. Once the user's identity has been verified, Organigram can use that certificate to generate advanced e-signatures for eligible procedures and records.

This is useful for:

- board, shareholder, member, or officer decisions
- approvals that require identity-backed evidence
- sensitive authorizations where a simple wallet signature is not enough
- organizations that need multiple users to sign on behalf of the entity

## Verifying your identity

To use advanced signatures, you must be subscribed to Certified Solo or covered by a Certified Entity workspace, then verify your identity.

You can go to Account Settings and click the "Verify your identity" button. You may be asked to provide a valid ID, a proof of address, and a selfie. The process is designed to take only a few minutes.

Once your identity is verified, your wallet can be used to generate advanced e-signatures for eligible procedures.

If you are part of a Certified Entity workspace, verified users of the organization can generate advanced e-signatures for the selected procedures in that organization.
